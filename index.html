Felix Noth

Professor of Banking and Financial Systems

Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH) · Otto-von-Guericke University Magdeburg

I am a Professor of Banking and Financial Systems at the Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH) and Otto-von-Guericke University in Magdeburg. I studied Economics at Ludwig-Maximilians University in Munich and did my Ph.D. in Finance at Goethe University, Frankfurt.

My research concerns topics related to financial systems' stability and financial intermediation. This website gives an overview of my research.
